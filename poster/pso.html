How the PSO Works

Governs fish movements and decision-making based on internal vitals.

Trajectories are calculated via 12 weighted vectors, prioritizing survival, health, and swarm intelligence.

cFood 3x Toward nearest food source when fullness < 100%
cProbiotic 1.5x Toward nearest probiotic when immunity < 100%
cOxygen 2x Toward nearest oxygen bubble when level < 70%
cSocial 1.5x Toward global swarm optima
cSelfish 1.5x Away from nearest fish to maintain space
cNH3 4x Away from nearest poison/ammonia area
cDisease 2.5x Away from nearest identified disease area
cParasite 3x Away from nearest parasite hotspot
cObstacle 2x Away from nearest physical pond obstacle
cRelief 4x Toward walls to scrub off parasites
cRun 5x Away from nearest larger mouthed fish
cWander 1x Toward pond center, random search if isolated

Vector priority shifts dynamically as Hunger, Oxygen, and Infection levels change.
