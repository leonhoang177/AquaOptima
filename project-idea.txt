We want to apply a combination of EA and PSO to a real-world problem in the fish production industry: optimizing multi-species aquaculture environments.

We are building a nested model where an inner PSO simulation handles dynamic fish foraging and group behavior, while an outer EA loop evaluates and evolves the tank's conditions.

Ultimately, this system will allow us to input a target fish population and diversity, and automatically output the precise environmental parameters required to sustain them, or it might be opposite.
